  • Recalculated all summaries from the cleaned records.

Limitation

Median imputation for missing ages is a simple practice technique. In a real project, the right treatment depends on business context and data-governance rules, and the reason for the missing values should be investigated first.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR A REAL REPORTING WORKFLOW

Monitor monthly volume

Use one consistent monthly report to spot workload changes and plan staffing or follow-up.

Track status ageing

Add days-in-current-status and a clear service-level target to separate normal processing from overdue cases.

Improve data entry controls

Dropdown lists, required fields, date validation, and unique record IDs prevent inconsistent values at the source.

Define KPI rules

Document what counts as “completed” and whether the completion-rate denominator includes rejected or withdrawn cases.

Review missing data

Find out why fields are empty before deciding to impute, exclude, or flag them.

Read results with care

Differences in counts or days are descriptive. They do not prove service quality or cause, and this data is randomly generated.
